# Campaign brief: Autumn 2026

**Objective:** Sales & conversion - increase autumn sales
**Dates:** 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-30 (30 days)
**Audience:** young professionals
**Promotion:** 15% off for the first 48 hours
**KPIs:** revenue, conversion rate, average order value, ROAS

## Products
- SKU-1003 Denim Jacket (Apparel, 79.00)
- SKU-1005 Canvas Tote Bag (Accessories, 12.50)
- SKU-1006 Running Shoes (42) (Footwear, 89.90)
- SKU-1007 Running Shoes (43) (Footwear, 89.90)
- SKU-1008 Wool Scarf (Accessories, 24.00)

## Messaging
**Headline:** Autumn 2026: made to be bought
**Tagline:** Sales & conversion campaign, 01 Nov - 30 Nov 2026
- 5 products across Accessories, Apparel, Footwear.
- Built from Canvas, Denim, Merino wool, Mesh.
- Prices from 12.50 to 89.90.
- Offer: 15% off for the first 48 hours.
- Made for young professionals.
**CTA:** Shop now

## Channels
- **Email & SMS** (Primary): Highest-converting owned channel. KPI: conversion rate, revenue per send
- **Paid search & shopping ads** (Primary): Captures high purchase intent. KPI: ROAS, CPA
- **Retargeting (paid social/display)** (Secondary): Brings back cart/product viewers. KPI: ROAS, assisted conversions
- **Onsite banners & product badges** (Secondary): Reinforces the offer at decision time. KPI: banner CTR, conversion
- **TikTok creators** (Secondary): Audience skews young and short-video native. KPI: views, saves
- **Promo code on landing page + checkout** (Supporting): Needed to redeem: 15% off for the first 48 hours. KPI: code redemption rate
